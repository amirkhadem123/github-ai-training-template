# Exercise 1 — Repository Orientation

## Goal

Become comfortable entering an unfamiliar repository without needing to understand the code.

## Before you change anything

Find:

- the README;
- the `input/` folder;
- the `output/` folder;
- the `scripts/` folder;
- the project template;
- `.gitignore`;
- `AGENTS.md`;
- `CLAUDE.md`.

Then ask your AI assistant:

> Review this repository and explain what it does at a level suitable for someone who does not write code. Identify the important files and folders, explain the input and output, and tell me how the workflow is run. Do not make any changes.

## Completion check

You should be able to explain:

- what goes into the workflow;
- what file runs the workflow;
- what the workflow produces;
- where the AI agent gets its project instructions.
